# Brain Resolver

Decide where to file every new page:

1. **people/** — Any person. Friends, family, colleagues, acquaintances.
2. **companies/** — Companies, organizations, projects-as-businesses.
3. **concepts/** — Abstract ideas, frameworks, terminology.
4. **meetings/** — Any meeting or conversation with structure.
5. **projects/** — Active projects, side projects, thesis work.
6. **originals/** — YOUR original thinking. Exact phrasing, vivid names.
7. **ideas/** — Product ideas, business ideas, feature concepts.
8. **sources/** — External references, links, bookmarks.
9. **learning/** — Learning notes, study materials, English learning.
