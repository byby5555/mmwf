Modern Instagram Reels are protected by aggressive anti-scraping measures. Every attempt to extract the content—whether via Instagram's API, direct page fetching, or third-party tools—resulted in lockouts, invalid tokens, or empty responses. This makes it nearly impossible for automated tools to analyze or summarize the content of a Reel without manual intervention or a specialized browser session.
