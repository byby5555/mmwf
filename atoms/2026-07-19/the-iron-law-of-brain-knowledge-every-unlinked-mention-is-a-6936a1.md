Garry Tan's brain-ops protocol includes a non-negotiable rule: every mention of a person or company that has a brain page MUST create a back-link from that entity's page to the page mentioning them. An unlinked reference is a 'broken brain.' This isomorphic link maintenance turns a passive archive into an active, traversable knowledge graph — where reading any entity page reveals every conversation, decision, or document that touched it.
