When attempting to extract metadata from an Instagram Reel link, Instagram's platform restrictions prevent direct scraping of detailed post data. The only metadata retrievable from search results is the basic description, like count, comment count, publisher username, and posting date. Full extraction would require official API access or specialized third-party tools, which themselves face limitations.
