Cobalt.tools is not a file sharing or temporary link service. It's an open-source media downloader that acts as a proxy between you and platforms like YouTube, TikTok, and Bilibili. It fetches media on your behalf, streams it through an encrypted tunnel, and processes remuxing/transcoding locally on your device to avoid storing anything on servers.
