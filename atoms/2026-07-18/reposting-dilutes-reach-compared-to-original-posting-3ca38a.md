Reposted Reels only reach 40-60% of what original posts achieve, according to Instagram's internal dynamics. This gap means that while reposting can keep your profile active, it cannot substitute genuine original creation if growth is the goal. Savvy creators should allocate reposts to fill gaps but reserve high-stakes content for first-run originals.
